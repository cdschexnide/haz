# Attachment 13 Inspection Criteria (Class 9)

This document maps Class 9 materials with packaging paragraphs starting with A13 to
inspection criteria derived from `docs/attachment13/A13.ts`. Criteria apply to each
material listed under the corresponding A13 paragraph.

## A13.2 - Class 9 materials (fertilizers, EHS, PCB, etc.)

Materials
- NA1350 - SULFUR
- NA3077 - HAZARDOUS WASTE, SOLID, N.O.S.
- NA3077 - OTHER REGULATED SUBSTANCES, SOLID, N.O.S.
- NA3082 - HAZARDOUS WASTE, LIQUID, N.O.S.
- NA3082 - OTHER REGULATED SUBSTANCES, LIQUID, N.O.S.
- NA3334 - SELF-DEFENSE SPRAY, NON-PRESSURIZED
- UN1931 - ZINC DITHIONITE
- UN1931 - ZINC HYDROSULFITE
- UN1941 - DIBROMODIFLUOROMETHANE, R12B2
- UN1990 - BENZALDEHYDE
- UN2071 - AMMONIUM NITRATE BASED FERTILIZER
- UN2216 - FISH MEAL, STABILIZED
- UN2216 - FISH SCRAP, STABILIZED
- UN2315 - POLYCHLORINATED BIPHENYLS, LIQUID
- UN2969 - CASTOR BEANS
- UN2969 - CASTOR FLAKE
- UN2969 - CASTOR MEAL
- UN2969 - CASTOR POMACE
- UN3077 - ENVIRONMENTALLY HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE, SOLID, N.O.S.
- UN3082 - ENVIRONMENTALLY HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE, LIQUID, N.O.S.
- UN3151 - HALOGENATED MONOMETHYLDIPHENYLMETHANES, LIQUID
- UN3151 - POLYHALOGENATED BIPHENYLS, LIQUID
- UN3151 - POLYHALOGENATED TERPHENYLS, LIQUID
- UN3152 - HALOGENATED MONOMETHYLDIPHENYLMETHANES, SOLID
- UN3152 - POLYHALOGENATED BIPHENYLS, SOLID
- UN3152 - POLYHALOGENATED TERPHENYLS, SOLID
- UN3432 - POLYCHLORINATED BIPHENYLS, SOLID

Required inspection criteria
- Handling: For UN1941, verify storage/handling avoids high temperatures and is in a cool,
  ventilated area away from flame; for UN3077/UN3082 technical name Otto Fuel II, verify
  leak precautions prevent skin contact, ingestion, or inhalation of vapors.
- Liquids: Verify Class 9 liquids are packaged per A13.2.2 in one of the allowed systems:
  combination packaging with approved inner receptacles and outer drums/barrels/jerricans/
  boxes, or single packaging drums/jerricans/barrels, or composite packagings with plastic
  or glass inner receptacles, or DOT cylinders where applicable.
- Solids: Verify Class 9 solids are packaged per A13.2.3 in one of the allowed systems:
  combination packaging with approved inner receptacles and outer drums/barrels/jerricans/
  boxes, or single packaging drums/barrels/jerricans/boxes/bags (bags not authorized for
  PG I), or composite packagings with plastic or glass inner receptacles, or DOT cylinders.
- Otto Fuel II exercise items: If applicable, confirm fired exercise torpedoes or rockets with
  Otto Fuel II are in original or authorized containers per Attachment 5.
- PCB transformers: If shipment is a large transformer with PCB, confirm palletization,
  tight sealing to prevent leakage, polyethylene sheeting under and up the sides, and
  absorbent material; UN specification packaging is not required.

## A13.3 - Consumer commodity

Materials
- ID8000 - CONSUMER COMMODITY

Required inspection criteria
- Scope: Confirm the commodity fits the allowed classes/UNs and has no subsidiary hazard.
- Limited quantity: Verify contents meet A19.3.2 limited quantity limits.
- Packaging: Use strong outer packaging meeting Attachment 3; UN specification packaging
  is not required.
- Weight: Final package gross mass does not exceed 30 kg.
- Drop test: If inner packagings are brittle/breakable, verify packaging can withstand a
  4 foot drop onto solid concrete.
- Aerosols (Class 2, non-toxic): Verify inner receptacle capacity limits, pressure limits at
  55 degrees C, proper receptacle spec when pressures exceed thresholds, heat test
  for larger aerosols, and valve protection by cap.
- Biological/medical aerosols: Verify lower pressure limit and heat test sampling.
- Liquids: Inner packagings not over 500 mL and not liquid-full at 55 degrees C.
- Solids: Inner packagings not over 500 g.

## A13.4 - Vehicles (flammable gas/liquid powered, fuel cell)

Materials
- UN3166 - VEHICLE, FLAMMABLE GAS POWERED
- UN3166 - VEHICLE, FLAMMABLE LIQUID POWERED
- UN3166 - VEHICLE, FUEL CELL, FLAMMABLE GAS POWERED
- UN3166 - VEHICLE, FUEL CELL, FLAMMABLE LIQUID POWERED

Required inspection criteria
- Technical manuals: Preparation follows the vehicle service/technical manual.
- Fuel limits: Each liquid fuel tank is no more than 1/2 full unless a listed exception applies;
  follow A13.4.2 conditions for draining/purging (ramp loading, palletized/trailers,
  freight container procedures, boats, helicopters/aircraft, UAVs, and airdrop limits).
- Freight container: Drain and purge when required; if no procedures, drain, run engine
  until stall, leave tanks/lines open 24 hours, and ensure installed batteries are non-spillable
  or non-regulated per A3.1.16.
- Gaseous fuel systems: Non-DOT pressurized vessels must be emptied; DOT cylinders
  may remain if shutoff valve is closed and lines/regulator are emptied.
- Fuel cell vehicles: Fuel cells protected from damage; proper description used.
- Accessorial hazards: Batteries secured upright, terminals protected, and acid/alkali batteries
  removed for freight container shipment per A12.4; no loose hazmat stored in vehicle racks
  unless authorized.

## A13.5 - UN3548 articles containing miscellaneous dangerous goods, N.O.S.

Materials
- UN3548 - ARTICLES CONTAINING MISCELLANEOUS DANGEROUS GOODS, N.O.S.

Required inspection criteria
- Classification: Confirm classification per A4.2.3 and max net quantity per package
  (60 L liquids, 100 kg solids).
- Packaged articles: Packaging meets Packing Group II performance; articles prevent movement
  and inadvertent operation; liquid closures oriented properly.
- Articles without inner receptacles: Article fully encloses dangerous goods and prevents
  release; outer packaging matches the allowed drum/box/jerrican types in A13.5.1.3.
- Robust articles: Either placed in strong outer packaging or shipped unpackaged/on pallets
  with equivalent protection.

## A13.6 - Battery-powered equipment and vehicles

Materials
- UN3171 - BATTERY-POWERED EQUIPMENT
- UN3171 - BATTERY-POWERED VEHICLE

Required inspection criteria
- Technical manuals: Use equipment/vehicle service technical manuals for preparation.
- Batteries: Secure upright in holders; protect terminals against short circuit; if not upright
  shipping is possible, remove battery and ship per A12.4.
- Installed equipment: Secure original installed equipment; do not store other hazmat in racks
  or containers.
- Wheelchairs (non-spillable): Protect terminals, secure battery to chair or remove and box.
- Wheelchairs (spillable): Keep upright, battery installed and secured, terminals protected,
  deactivate power; remove battery if not upright shipment.
- Lithium batteries: Securely fasten in holders and protect terminals; prototype/low production
  batteries must pass UN tests or DOT approval.

## A13.7 - Lithium metal and lithium ion batteries

Materials
- UN3090 - LITHIUM METAL BATTERIES (including lithium alloy batteries)
- UN3480 - LITHIUM ION BATTERIES (including lithium polymer batteries)

Required inspection criteria
- Compliance: Meet A3.3.9.2 except A3.3.9.2.3.
- Packaging: Non-metallic inner packaging fully enclosing cells/batteries; separation from
  conductive materials; outer packaging types per A13.7.2.1 with PG II performance.
- Heavy batteries: Over 12 kg may use strong outer packaging or pallets; secure to prevent
  movement; terminals do not bear weight; identify as P4 for passenger movement.
- Large packaging: Approved large packagings per A13.7.2.3.
- Segregation: UN3480/UN3090 not packaged or overpacked with Class 1 (except 1.4S),
  Division 2.1, Class 3, Division 4.1, or Division 5.1 materials.

## A13.8 - Lithium batteries contained in equipment (including CTU)

Materials
- UN3091 - LITHIUM METAL BATTERIES CONTAINED IN EQUIPMENT (including lithium alloy batteries)
- UN3481 - LITHIUM ION BATTERIES CONTAINED IN EQUIPMENT (including lithium polymer batteries)
- UN3536 - LITHIUM BATTERIES INSTALLED IN A CARGO TRANSPORT UNIT (lithium ion batteries or lithium metal batteries)

Required inspection criteria
- Compliance: Meet A3.3.9.2 except A3.3.9.2.3.
- Packaging: UN specification packaging not required; equipment secured in strong outer
  packaging unless equipment provides equivalent protection.
- Movement/short circuit: Prevent movement, accidental operation, and short circuits.
- Additional batteries: If additional cells/batteries are present, package them per A13.7.2.
- Marking: If cells/batteries are in equipment and also packed with equipment, mark package
  with the correct proper shipping name for packed with equipment.
- Vehicles/equipment: Secure batteries in holders and protect terminals.
- Airdrop missions: Equipment may be hand carried in rucksack, airdrop container, or door
  bundle; no Shipper's Declaration required.

## A13.9 - Lithium batteries packed with equipment

Materials
- UN3091 - LITHIUM METAL BATTERIES PACKED WITH EQUIPMENT (including lithium alloy batteries)
- UN3481 - LITHIUM ION BATTERIES PACKED WITH EQUIPMENT (including lithium polymer batteries)

Required inspection criteria
- Compliance: Meet A3.3.9.2 except A3.3.9.2.3.
- Packaging: Inner packagings fully enclose cells/batteries and prevent short circuits;
  outer packagings meet PG II performance per A13.7.2.
- Large packaging: Approved large packagings per A13.9.2.3.
- Airdrop missions: Equipment may be hand carried in rucksack, airdrop container, or door
  bundle; no Shipper's Declaration required.

## A13.10 - Carbon dioxide, solid (dry ice)

Materials
- UN1845 - CARBON DIOXIDE, DRY ICE
- UN1845 - CARBON DIOXIDE, SOLID

Required inspection criteria
- Handling: Confirm storage in well-ventilated areas; no hermetically sealed containers; avoid
  contact hazards; maximize ventilation during ground operations.
- Packaging: Wrap in kraft paper, secure with tape, and pack in fiberboard boxes, polystyrene
  foam containers, or other suitable packaging that permits CO2 release and prevents
  pressure buildup; UN specification packaging is not required.
- Medical shipments: If applicable, confirm preparation per DLAR 4145.21/TB MED 284/
  NAVSUPINST 4610.31A.
- Non-hazard shipments with dry ice: Use vented packaging as above.

## A13.11 - Magnetized material

Materials
- UN2807 - MAGNETIZED MATERIAL

Required inspection criteria
- Handling: Maintain 4.6 m (15 ft) separation from compass sensing devices or sensitive
  equipment during storage.
- Field strength: Verify shielding reduces field to 5.25 milligauss or less, or compass
  deviation is 2 degrees or less at 4.6 m; measure with operational meters, ideally two.
- Packaging: Provide blocking and bracing; package magnetic tubes individually per MIL-E-75;
  ensure protective distance between magnetic surface and container outside is at least
  102 mm (4 inches).
- Eligibility: Materials with field strength over 0.00525 gauss at 4.6 m are forbidden for air
  movement; UN specification packaging is not required.

## A13.12 - Life-saving appliances

Materials
- UN2990 - LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES, SELF INFLATING
- UN3072 - LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES, NOT SELF INFLATING (containing dangerous goods as equipment)

Required inspection criteria
- Handling: Store cool, well-ventilated, away from heat/ignition; no dropping or rough handling.
- Packaging: Weather-resistant fiberboard or strong outer container; inner packaging prevents
  accidental activation; cushion to prevent movement; meet A3.1; UN specification packaging
  is not required.
- Individually assigned kits: If hand carried, use strong outer container or A-3 bag, crew
  informs Air Terminal Operations Center, storage per aircraft commander; while in possession,
  no other requirements apply.

## A13.13 - Dangerous goods in apparatus or machinery

Materials
- UN3363 - DANGEROUS GOODS IN APPARATUS
- UN3363 - DANGEROUS GOODS IN MACHINERY

Required inspection criteria
- Contents: Only limited quantities per A19.3, authorized magnetized material, or Division
  2.2 gases without subsidiary hazard (no refrigerated liquefied gases).
- Compatibility: Multiple materials may not react dangerously together.
- Quantity limits: Per package max 1 kg solids, 500 mL liquids, 0.5 kg Class 2.2 gases.
- Receptacles: Secure/cushion to prevent breakage/leakage; cushioning must not react with
  contents.
- Leakage control: No leakage possible if receptacle fails; leak-proof liner for drained but not
  purged articles; seal/cap all openings and lines.
- Gases: Class 2.2 gases in authorized cylinders per Attachment 6.
- Outer packaging: Strong outer packaging unless item construction provides equivalent
  protection; UN specification packaging is not required.

## A13.14 - Class 9 materials (general)

Materials
- UN1841 - ACETALDEHYDE AMMONIA
- UN3334 - AVIATION REGULATED LIQUID, N.O.S.
- UN3335 - AVIATION REGULATED SOLID, N.O.S.

Required inspection criteria
- Packaging: Use appropriate non-bulk packaging meeting Attachment 3; UN specification
  packaging is not required.
- Outage: For packagings 208 L (55 gallon) or less, provide enough outage so the packaging
  is not liquid-full at 54 degrees C.
- Vapor pressure: If absolute vapor pressure exceeds 110 kPa at 38 degrees C, ensure the
  primary packaging withstands internal pressure at 54 degrees C without leakage.
- Water-reactive: If water-reactive, use inner or outer waterproof packaging.

## A13.15 - Air bag inflators/modules and seat-belt pretensioners

Materials
- UN3268 - SAFETY DEVICES (electrically initiated)

Required inspection criteria
- Packaging: Pack in boxes, drums, or jerricans using the allowed types in A13.15
  (boxes 4A/4B/4C1/4C2/4D/4F/4G/4H1/4H2/4N; drums 1A2/1B2/1D/1G/1H2/1N2;
  jerricans 3A2/3B2/3H2).

## A13.16 - Asbestos (hydrated mineral silicates)

Materials
- NA2212 - ASBESTOS
- UN2212 - ASBESTOS, AMPHIBOLE (amosite, tremolite, actinolite, anthopphylite, or crocidolite)
- UN2590 - ASBESTOS, CHRYSOTILE
- UN2590 - WHITE ASBESTOS (anthophyllite)

Required inspection criteria
- Exposure control: Loading/handling minimizes airborne particle exposure; decontamination
  of aircraft if needed.
- Packaging: Meet A3.1 general requirements; UN specification packaging is not required.
- Packaging options: Rigid leak-tight drums; dust/sift-proof bags palletized and unitized; or
  dust/sift-proof bags in strong outer fiberboard/wooden boxes.

## A13.17 - Polymeric beads, expandable and plastic molding compound

Materials
- UN2211 - POLYMERIC BEADS, EXPANDABLE (evolving flammable vapor)
- UN3314 - PLASTIC MOULDING COMPOUND (in dough, sheet, or extruded rope form evolving flammable vapor)

Required inspection criteria
- Inner packaging: Sealed plastic liner.
- Outer packaging: Boxes (4A/4B/4C1/4C2/4D/4F/4G/4H1/4H2/4N) or drums (1D/1G).
- Alternate: Vapor-tight metal or plastic drums (1A1/1A2/1B1/1B2/1H1/1H2/1N1/1N2)
  allowed without liner.

## A13.18 - Chemical kit and first aid kit

Materials
- UN3316 - CHEMICAL KIT
- UN3316 - FIRST AID KIT

Required inspection criteria
- Contents: Kit PG equals most stringent PG of any substance; contents packed to prevent
  dangerous reactions; only limited quantities per A19.3.2 and excepted quantities per
  A19.2 with inner packaging per A19.2.3.
- Inner receptacles: Max 250 mL liquids or 250 g solids (Division 5.2 Type D/E/F: max
  125 mL liquids or 250 g solids).
- Totals: Per kit max 1 L liquids or 1 kg solids; per package max 10 kg total dangerous goods.
- Outer packaging: Protect inner receptacles and use box types listed in A13.18.2.3.
- Limited quantities: Refer to Table A19.2 Note 1 when applicable.

## A13.19 - Capacitors

Materials
- UN3499 - CAPACITOR, ELECTRIC DOUBLE LAYER (with an energy storage capacity greater than 0.3 Wh)
- UN3508 - CAPACITOR, ASYMMETRIC (with an energy storage capacity greater than 0.3 Wh)

Required inspection criteria
- Charge state: Capacitors not installed in equipment are shipped uncharged.
- Short circuit protection: Protect terminals; if energy storage is 10 Wh or less, protect
  against short circuit or fit metal strap; over 10 Wh requires metal strap.
- Pressure/venting: Hazardous-electrolyte capacitors withstand 95 kPa differential; provide
  safe pressure relief and contain released liquid.
- Marking: Capacitors marked with energy storage capacity in Wh.
- Packaging: Securely cushion and pack in strong outer packaging; equipment-installed
  capacitors may be shipped unpackaged if equipment protects them.
- Exemptions: Non-hazardous electrolyte capacitors only require A13.19.1; hazardous
  electrolyte with 10 Wh or less may be exempt if they pass a 1.2 m drop test; hazardous
  electrolyte over 10 Wh and not installed are subject to full requirements.

## A13.20 - UN3530 internal combustion engines/machinery

Materials
- UN3530 - ENGINE, INTERNAL COMBUSTION
- UN3530 - MACHINERY, INTERNAL COMBUSTION

Required inspection criteria
- Technical manuals: Prepare per equipment service technical manual.
- Fuel limits: Drain to 500 mL residual in tanks/lines (or as required by manual); special
  exceptions for wheeled equipment under Chapter 3 and for units with large fuel systems.
- Drain/purge: Drain to maximum extent for damaged/inoperable engines; cap and cover
  openings; drained and purged engines with no other hazmat are non-hazardous.
- Orientation: Secure in strong, rigid outer packaging if orientation could change and cause
  leakage or damage.
- Freight containers: Drain fuel, purge if required or if flash point under 38 degrees C; if no
  procedures, drain, run engine until stall, leave open 24 hours, ensure batteries are
  non-spillable or non-regulated.
- Spill risk: If unit is susceptible to spills per A3.3.3.6, drain and cap.
- Accessorial hazards: Batteries upright in holders, terminals protected; remove acid/alkali
  batteries for freight containers per A12.4; non-spillable batteries may remain if upright and
  disconnected.
