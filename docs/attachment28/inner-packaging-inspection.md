# Inner Packaging Inspection Procedures - Combination Packaging

**Source:** AFMAN24-604 Attachment 28 - Inspection Procedures
**Date:** 9 October 2020

## Overview

This document details the procedures for opening external containers to inspect the internal packaging configuration of combination packaging, and the procedures for closing those same external containers.

---

## Inspection Areas for Combination Packaging

**Reference:** A28.2.1.2

When inspecting combination packaging, the following areas must be verified:

1. **Inner receptacle orientation**
2. **Inner receptacle ullage** (headspace)
3. **Inner receptacle secondary closure**
4. **Absorbent and cushioning material**
5. **Leak-proof liner** (covering item or lining outer container)
6. **Air-eligible** marking
7. **External package markings** including:
   - UN specification code
   - Air-eligible marking
   - Hazard and handling marking/labels
   - Orientation markings for combination packagings and drums used as overpacks

---

## Opening External Containers to Inspect Internal Packaging

### General Principle

**Reference:** A28.2.2

The following instructions provide acceptable procedures for opening external containers to inspect the internal packaging configuration. **Comply with these procedures to maintain the performance capability of the package and the original shipper's certification.**

⚠️ **CRITICAL:** Noncompliance with any of these procedures constitutes repacking and requires a new certification.

Design inspection procedures to validate safety of the shipment. **Do not physically damage the package or perform any function that adversely affects the integrity or original performance capability of the packaging.**

---

### Fiberboard Box Opening

**Reference:** A28.2.2.1

#### Procedures:

1. Cut original tape along seam using a shallow blade knife
2. **Do not tear tape**

#### Warnings:

⚠️ If adhesive sealed on inside box flaps or the flaps are stitched/stapled (not closed by tape), opening may damage packaging components.

---

### Wood Box Opening

**Reference:** A28.2.2.3

#### Procedures:

1. Opening causes damage to packaging material
2. To reduce damage to wood material, use a nail puller to remove nails
3. **Do not pry open wood box panels using crowbars, etc.**

---

### Drum Opening

**Reference:** A28.2.2.5

#### Restrictions:

- ✅ Only open drums used as a **combination package or overpack**
- ❌ **Do not open drums used as a single package for liquid hazardous material**

---

### Overpacks

**Reference:** A28.2.2.7.1

#### Procedures:

- Outer packaging used as an "Overpack" (for ease of handling) may be opened for inspection of contents
- Follow inspection guidance for specific opening and closing of inside shipping containers according to A28.2.2

---

## Inner Package Inspection

**Reference:** A28.2.3

### Visual Inspection Procedures:

1. **Perform visual inspection only**
2. **Do not rearrange inner packaging contents or configuration**
3. **Do not cut wraps or barrier material**

### Critical Rule:

⚠️ **Any change to the inner configuration is considered repacking and requires a new shipper's certification.**

---

## Closing External Containers After Inspection

### Fiberboard Box Closure

**Reference:** A28.2.2.2

#### Procedures:

1. Apply new tape over the existing tape using same method as original
2. Use only **ASTM D 5486, Type I, Class 2** tape
   - Film backed
   - Pressure-sensitive adhesive
   - Weather resistant
3. Ensure the ends of sealing tape extends over the original tape a minimum of **one-inch** adhering to the fiberboard on the ends of the package
4. Use **three-inch wide tape** OR **two strips of two-inch wide tape**
5. Ensure surface is **clean and dry** before applying tape
6. Ensure box flaps meet squarely
7. **Do not cover markings or labels with tape**

#### Certification Requirements:

| Closure Method | New Certification Required? |
|----------------|---------------------------|
| Tape-closed box, reclosed per procedures above | ❌ **NO** - Packaging is considered returned to original condition |
| Box with adhesive sealed inside flaps | ✅ **YES** - Reclosure is considered repacking |
| Box with stitched/stapled flaps | ✅ **YES** - Reclosure is considered repacking |

**Reference:** A28.2.2.2.7, A28.2.2.2.8

💡 **Note:** When reclosed using these procedures, a new shipper's certification is NOT required. Based on DOD testing, the packaging is considered returned to original condition and is not considered repacking.

---

### Wood Box Closure

**Reference:** A28.2.2.4

#### Procedures:

1. **Do not close by nailing through existing holes**
2. Replace damaged components
3. Use prescribed materials and specifications required by:
   - Applicable test report
   - Special packaging instruction (SPI)
   - Drawing

#### Certification Requirements:

⚠️ **Replacing packaging material components is considered repacking and requires a new shipper's certification.**

---

### Drum Closure

**Reference:** A28.2.2.6

#### Procedures:

1. **Replace old gaskets with new gaskets and seals**
   - Old gaskets may "set" and not reseal properly
2. Use the torque and closing instructions required by the applicable test report

#### Certification Requirements:

⚠️ **Reclosure of drum is considered repacking and requires new shipper's certification.**

---

### Overpacks Closure

**Reference:** A28.2.2.7.2

#### Procedures:

- Close overpacks in a similar manner as received

#### Certification Requirements:

✅ **A new shipper's declaration is NOT required**

---

## Non-Specification (Strong Outside) Packaging

**Reference:** A28.2.2.8

### Opening:
- Non-specification packaging may be opened for inspection

### Closure:
- Close non-specification packaging in a similar manner as received
- ✅ **A new shipper's declaration is NOT required**

---

## UN Specification Jerricans

**Reference:** A28.2.2.9

### Opening:
- Caps may be removed for inspection

### Closure:
- Re-secure cap (hand-tight) ensuring there is no "cross-threading"
- ✅ **A new shipper's declaration is NOT required**

---

## Shrink Wrap Packages

**Reference:** A28.2.2.10

### Critical Restrictions:

❌ **Do not cut, tear, or remove stretch or shrink wrap to verify packaging**

### Rejection Criteria:

Reject shipments if stretch or shrink wrap is cut, torn, or damaged so that it would:
- Prevent packages containing liquid hazardous materials from tipping or becoming loose in flight
- Present any package that would be a hazard during handling operations

---

## Exceptions to Inspection

**Reference:** A28.2.4

Some item packaging requires **specialized training** for opening, interior inspection, and closure. **Only individuals trained and qualified in these specialized areas are authorized to open the following packagings:**

1. ☢️ **Radioactive material**
2. 💣 **Class 1 (ammunition and explosives)**
3. 🦠 **Etiological Agents or Infectious Substances**
4. 🗜️ **Pressurized metal shipping containers or drums**
5. ☠️ **Material identified as "inhalation hazard"**

---

## Quick Reference: New Certification Required?

| Container Type | Action | New Certification Required? |
|----------------|--------|---------------------------|
| Fiberboard box (tape-closed) | Reclose per procedures | ❌ NO |
| Fiberboard box (adhesive/stapled) | Reclose | ✅ YES |
| Wood box | Replace components | ✅ YES |
| Drum | Reclose (replace gaskets) | ✅ YES |
| Overpack | Reclose in similar manner | ❌ NO |
| Non-specification packaging | Reclose in similar manner | ❌ NO |
| UN Specification Jerricans | Re-secure cap | ❌ NO |
| Inner configuration | Any change | ✅ YES |

## Key Principles Summary

### When Opening:
1. ✅ Use appropriate tools (shallow blade knife, nail puller)
2. ❌ Do not damage packaging components
3. ❌ Do not pry or force open
4. ⚠️ Know when specialized training is required

### During Inspection:
1. ✅ Perform visual inspection only
2. ❌ Do not rearrange inner packaging
3. ❌ Do not cut wraps or barrier material
4. ❌ Do not compromise package integrity

### When Closing:
1. ✅ Use proper materials (specified tape, new gaskets)
2. ✅ Follow torque specifications when applicable
3. ✅ Ensure clean, dry surfaces
4. ✅ Maintain original configuration
5. ❌ Do not cover markings or labels
6. ⚠️ Know when new certification is required
