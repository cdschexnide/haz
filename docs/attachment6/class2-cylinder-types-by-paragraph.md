# AFMAN 24-604 Attachment 6: Valid Cylinder Types by Packaging Paragraph

This document lists the valid cylinder types and container specifications for each Class 2 (Compressed Gases) packaging paragraph from A6.2 through A6.28.

---

## A6.2 - Aerosols

### Valid Container Types
| Container | Pressure Range | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| DOT 2P | 970-1105 kPa at 55°C (140-160 psig at 130°F) | Inner metal receptacle |
| DOT 2Q | 1105-1245 kPa at 55°C (160-180 psig at 130°F) | Inner metal receptacle |
| ICAO/IATA IP7 | 970-1105 kPa at 55°C | |
| ICAO/IATA IP7A | 970-1245 kPa at 55°C | |
| ICAO/IATA IP7B | 970-1500 kPa at 55°C (up to 217 psig at 130°F) | Only option for 1245-1500 kPa range |

### Outer Packaging (for toxic aerosols)
- 4G (Fiberboard box)
- 4C1 (Ordinary natural wood box)
- 4C2 (Sift-proof natural wood box)
- 4D (Plywood box)
- 4F (Reconstituted wood box)
- 4H1, 4H2 (Plastic boxes)

### Restrictions
- Package gross weight must not exceed 30 kg (66 lbs)
- Must withstand 1.5x equilibrium pressure at 55°C without bursting
- Non-refillable non-metal receptacles limited to 120 mL (4 fl oz)
- Non-refillable metal or plastic receptacles limited to 1 L (34 fl oz)

## A6.8 - Refrigerating Machines, Air Conditioners, Pressurized Hydraulic/Pneumatic Articles

### Valid Container Types
**No DOT specification cylinders required** - Factory-tested units are exempt from specification packaging requirements.

### Requirements
- Pressure vessels per ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 15, or ASME Code (if >152.4 mm internal diameter)
- Safety relief device per ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 15
- Group A1 refrigerant: max 2268 kg (5,000 lbs) per vessel
- Other refrigerants: max 22.7 kg (50 lbs) per vessel
- Hydraulic/pneumatic articles: test to 3x charged pressure (min 830 kPa/120 psig)

---

## A6.10 - Cigarette Lighters and Similar Devices

### Valid Container Types
- Inner packaging designed to prevent movement and inadvertent ignition
- Any rigid non-bulk UN specification outer packaging (PG II level)

### Restrictions
- Max 10 grams (0.35 fl oz) liquefied gas per device
- Liquid portion ≤85% volumetric capacity at 15°C
- Must withstand 2x vapor pressure at 55°C
- Lighter refills: max 4 fl oz (7.22 cu in) or 65g Division 2.1 fuel

## A6.22 - Chemical Under Pressure N.O.S.

### Valid Cylinder Types
Per A6.4 (Liquefied) and A6.5 (Nonliquefied):
- DOT 3, 3A, 3AA, 3AL, 3B, 3BN, 3E
- DOT 4B, 4BA, 4B240ET, 4BW, 4E, 39
- UN pressure receptacles

### Requirements
- Min test pressure: 291 psig (20 bar)
- Internal pressure at 65°C ≤ cylinder test pressure
- At 50°C: non-gaseous phase ≤95% water capacity
- Not completely filled at 60°C
- Max requalification period: 5 years

## A6.24 - Fuel Cell Cartridges Contained in Equipment

### Container Requirements
- **UN specification packaging NOT required**
- Fuel cells must be protected against short circuit
- System protected against inadvertent operation
- Fuel cell systems may NOT charge batteries during transport

---

## A6.25 - Fuel Cell Packed With Equipment

### Container Requirements
- **UN specification packaging NOT required**
- Pack in inner packagings OR outer packaging with cushioning/dividers
- Max cartridges: equipment requirement + 2 spares

---

## A6.26 - Metal Hydride Storage Systems

### Valid Container Types
- UN Metal hydride storage systems (UN3468)
- Pressure receptacles per ISO 16111

### Requirements
- Max water capacity: 150 L (40 gal)
- Max developed pressure: 25 MPa
- Steel/composite steel receptacles: "H" mark for hydrogen-bearing gases
- Requalification: max 5-year intervals per ISO 16111

---

## A6.27 - Flammable Gas Powered Engines and Machinery

### Container Requirements
- Strong, rigid outer packaging (if handled non-upright)
- Non-DOT specification fuel tanks allowed but must be completely emptied
- Batteries secured upright in designed holders
- When in freight container: remove acid/alkali batteries, package per A12.4

---

## A6.28 - Articles Containing Flammable Gas N.O.S.

### Valid Outer Packaging (PG II)
| Type | Specifications |
|------|----------------|
| **Drums** | 1A2, 1B2, 1N2, 1D, 1G, 1H2 |
| **Jerricans** | 3A2, 3B2, 3H2 |
| **Boxes** | 4A, 4B, 4C1, 4C2, 4D, 4F, 4G, 4H1, 4H2, 4N |

### Requirements
- Max net quantity: 150 kg per package
- Pack to prevent movement and inadvertent operation

- Robust articles may be transported unpackaged or on pallets
