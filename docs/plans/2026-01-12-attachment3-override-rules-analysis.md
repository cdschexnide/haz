# AFMAN 24-604 Attachment 3 - Override Rules Analysis

**Analysis Date:** 2026-01-12
**Source Document:** AFMAN 24-604, 9 October 2020, Attachment 3 (pages 53-102)
**Purpose:** Document all override rules that modify, supersede, or supplement class-specific requirements

---

## Executive Summary

**Total Override Rules Identified:** 127+

### Most Impactful Categories:
1. **Packing Group Performance Upgrades** - 5 major rules affecting Classes 4, 5, 8, and materials with subsidiary hazards
2. **Aircraft Prohibitions** - Absolute prohibition on magnetized material; multiple radioactive restrictions
3. **UN Specification Exemptions** - 9 categories exempt from UN performance testing
4. **Fuel Draining/Purging** - Flash point-based requirements that override technical manual instructions
5. **Lithium Battery Exceptions** - Detailed exemption criteria that remove regulatory requirements

### Critical Rules Every Inspector Must Know:
1. **A3.1.9 (T-0)**: PG III with 4.1/4.2/4.3/5.1/8 subsidiary → MUST use PG II packaging
2. **A3.3.9.3**: Magnetized material >0.00525 gauss at 4.5m is **FORBIDDEN** on military aircraft
3. **A3.3.3.5.1**: Bulk tanks with flash point <38°C must be PURGED (not just drained)
4. **A3.1.7.1/A3.1.7.2**: Air-eligible pressure requirements override ground transport standards
5. **A3.3.7.12.3**: Radioactive + Division 2.1 subsidiary forbidden on passenger aircraft

## Section 1: Packing Group Performance Upgrades

These rules require a HIGHER packing group performance level than the material's assigned packing group.

### A3.1.9 - General PG III Upgrade (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.9
**Exact Text:** "A packaging containing a Packing Group III material with a primary or subsidiary hazard of Class/Division 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 5.1, or 8 must meet Packing Group II performance level."
**Affected Classes:** Any class with subsidiary 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 5.1, or 8
**Required Upgrade:** PG III → PG II
**Waiver Tier:** T-0 (No waiver authority)
**Impact:** A "Z" marked package (PG III only) is NOT acceptable; must use "Y" or "X" marked package

### A3.3.1.1.4 - Class 1 Explosives
**Paragraph:** A3.3.1.1.4
**Exact Text:** "Package all Class 1 material in packaging that meets the PG I or II performance level."
**Affected Classes:** All Class 1 (Explosives)
**Required Upgrade:** Any PG → PG I or II
**Waiver Tier:** Not specified (implied T-0 for safety)
**Impact:** No PG III packaging for any explosives

### A3.3.4.2 - Class 4 PG III Upgrade
**Paragraph:** A3.3.4.2
**Exact Text:** "Unless otherwise specified by a packaging paragraph, package a material identified as PG III in Table A4.1. in a container that meets the PG I or II performance level."
**Affected Classes:** Class 4 (Flammable Solids, Spontaneously Combustible, Dangerous When Wet)
**Required Upgrade:** PG III → PG I or II
**Waiver Tier:** Not specified
**Impact:** Class 4 PG III materials require PG I or II packaging

### A3.3.5.3 - Class 5 PG III Upgrade
**Paragraph:** A3.3.5.3
**Exact Text:** "Unless otherwise specified by a packaging paragraph, package a material identified as PG III in Table A4.1. in a container that meets the PG I or II performance level."
**Affected Classes:** Class 5 (Oxidizers, Organic Peroxides)
**Required Upgrade:** PG III → PG I or II
**Waiver Tier:** Not specified
**Impact:** Class 5 PG III materials require PG I or II packaging

### A3.3.8.2 - Class 8 Liquid PG III Upgrade
**Paragraph:** A3.3.8.2
**Exact Text:** "Unless otherwise specified by a packaging paragraph, package a liquid material identified as PG III in Table A4.1 in a container that meets the PG I or II performance level."
**Affected Classes:** Class 8 Liquids (Corrosives)
**Required Upgrade:** PG III liquids → PG I or II
**Waiver Tier:** Not specified
**Impact:** Class 8 liquid PG III materials require PG I or II packaging (solids not affected)

## Section 2: Pressure/Temperature Requirements

### A3.1.2.1 - Environmental Protection Requirements (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.2.1
**Requirements:**
- Temperature: -40 to +65.5°C (-40 to +150°F)
- Pressure (altitude): Sea level to 3.7 km (12,000 feet)
- Explosive decompression: 3.7 to 15.24 km (12,000 to 50,000 feet)
**Waiver Tier:** T-0
**Impact:** All packaging must withstand these conditions without deformation, leakage, or rupture

### A3.1.5 - Ullage (Outage) Requirements
**Paragraph:** A3.1.5
**Exact Text:** "For flammable liquids and other volatile liquids with a high coefficient of expansion, a minimum outage of 2 percent at 54 degrees C (130 degrees F), is required."
**Affected Materials:** Flammable liquids, volatile liquids
**Requirement:** Minimum 2% outage at 54°C
**Impact:** Containers cannot be filled completely

### A3.1.7.1 - Combination Packaging Pressure Standard (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.7.1
**Requirements:**
| Material Type | Minimum Pressure |
|--------------|------------------|
| Standard liquids/semi-solids | 95 kPa (14 psi) |
| PG III Class 3 or 6.1 | 75 kPa (11 psi) |
| Higher vapor pressure materials | Calculated per A3.1.7.1.1-A3.1.7.1.3 |

**Waiver Tier:** T-0
**Impact:** Inner packagings must withstand internal air gauge pressure testing

### A3.1.7.2 - Single/Composite Packaging Pressure Requirement (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.7.2
**Requirements:**
| Packing Group | Minimum Pressure |
|--------------|------------------|
| PG I | 250 kPa (36 psi) |
| PG III Class 3 or 6.1 | 80 kPa (12 psi) |
| All other liquids | 100 kPa (15 psi) |

**Waiver Tier:** T-0
**Impact:** Single packagings must meet hydraulic pressure test per 49 CFR 178.605

### A3.3.2.6 - Cylinder Pressure Requirements
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.6
**Key Rules:**
- **A3.3.2.6.2:** Pressure at 55°C must not exceed 1.25x service pressure (except acetylene, nitrous oxide, carbon dioxide) (T-0)
- **A3.3.2.6.3:** Hazard Zone A/B cylinders must not exceed service pressure at 55°C; must provide outage to not be liquid full at 55°C (T-0)
- **A3.3.2.6.6:** Liquefied gas must not completely fill packaging up to 54°C (except CO2, R-1132A, nitrous oxide, vinyl fluoride)

### A3.3.2.16 - Cryogenic Liquid Requirements
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.16.1
**Key Requirements:**
- **A3.3.2.16.1.1:** Cannot load cryogenic liquid colder than design service temperature
- **A3.3.2.16.1.3:** Flammable cryogenic liquid jacket MUST be steel (T-0)
- **A3.3.2.16.1.5:** Aluminum valves/pipes/fittings PROHIBITED for flammable cryogenics
- **A3.3.2.16.1.8:** Maximum weight cannot exceed design weight for temp colder than -195.5°C (-320°F)
- **A3.3.2.16.1.9:** Must have pressure control system per 49 CFR 173.316 to prevent liquid full condition (T-0)

## Section 3: SDDG Key Modifications

### A3.3.1.10 - Grandfathered Explosives (Key 19)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.1.10
**Requirement:** Annotate Key 19 with "Government-owned goods packaged before 1 January 1990."
**Affected Materials:** Government-owned Class 1 packaged before 1 January 1990
**Impact:** Exempts from UN specification requirements but requires Key 19 annotation

### A3.3.2.16.2 - Cryogenic Venting Instructions (Key 19)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.16.2
**Requirement:** "The shipper must provide required equipment and specific venting instructions in the additional handling information block of the Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods (see A17.5.2.), unless venting procedures are provided in a separate instruction accompanying the shipment or attached to the cargo." (T-0)
**Affected Materials:** Cryogenic liquids
**Impact:** Venting instructions mandatory in SDDG Key 19 or separate document

### A3.3.1.4 - Explosives Classification Documentation
**Paragraph:** A3.3.1.4
**Requirement:** Unless listed in JHCS, classification approval document must accompany shipment (T-0)
**Exception:** Not required for 1.4S munitions meeting A3.3.1.4.7 criteria
**Coalition Forces Documentation Must Include:**
- PSN
- UN number
- Hazard Class/Division
- Compatibility Group
- NEW or net explosive mass
- Indication whether mass is per article or per package

### A3.3.3.3.2 - Jerricans as Accessorial Hazard (Key 19)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.3.2
**Text:** "Secure jerricans in permanently configured and approved holders on vehicles or equipment. If secured in this manner, they may be considered an accessorial hazard, and included in Key 19 of the Shipper's Declaration of Dangerous Goods."

### A3.3.7.12.2 - Radioactive Subsidiary Hazard Description
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.12.2
**Requirement:** "The basic description required on the Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods must include a description of these subsidiary hazards (e.g., '3, 6.1'), the name of the constituents which most predominantly contribute to the subsidiary hazard(s), and where applicable, the packing group." (T-0)

## Section 4: Marking Requirement Modifications

### A3.1.16.4 - Empty/Nonhazardous Marking
**Paragraph:** A3.1.16.4
**Requirements:**
- Annotate "NONHAZARDOUS" in MSL address block and/or mark container "Non-Regulated"
- Apply "EMPTY" label per Attachment 15 when applicable
- Marking not required when hazardous contents completely removed AND no residue possible AND markings/labels removed or covered

### A3.3.7.12.1 - Radioactive Subsidiary Hazard Marking Exception
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.12.1
**Exception:** UN2908, UN2909, UN2910, UN2911, UN2977, UN2978 are EXCEPTED from subsidiary hazard marking/labeling requirements
**Impact:** These specific radioactive materials do not require subsidiary hazard labels even if they have subsidiary hazards

### A3.3.9.2.3.5 - Lithium Battery Mark
**Paragraph:** A3.3.9.2.3.5
**Requirement:** Mark each package with lithium battery mark per A14.4.8.5
**Exceptions:**
- Button cell batteries installed in equipment (including circuit boards)
- No more than 4 lithium cells or 2 lithium batteries installed in equipment
**Note:** "Markings do not prohibit the movement of passengers on military or contracted cargo aircraft."

### A3.3.9.6 - Dry Ice Net Weight Marking
**Paragraph:** Referenced in A12.14
**Requirement:** Mark net weight of dry ice on package
**Impact:** Additional marking beyond standard Class 9 requirements

### A3.1.16.4.4 - Cylinder Empty Identification
**Paragraph:** A3.1.16.4.4
**Requirement:** Identify cylinders as empty per A15.3.4
**Impact:** Specific empty marking requirements for cylinders

### A3.1.7.3 - Supplementary Packaging Requirements
**Paragraph:** A3.1.7.3
**Requirement:** Pack containers holding liquids that don't meet air transport pressure requirements into supplementary UN packaging that does meet requirement
**Restriction:** "Do not pack pressurized containers in sealed metal drums."

### A3.3.3.3.3 - DOT 5L Jerrican Prohibition (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.3.3
**Text:** "DOT 5L jerricans are not authorized for air shipment of fuel, and must be drained to the greatest extent possible."
**Waiver Tier:** T-0
**Impact:** Absolute prohibition on DOT 5L for air shipment of fuel

### A3.3.2.10 - Non-DOT Specification Cylinder Authorization (Expansion)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.10
**Authorized Non-DOT Cylinders:**
- A3.3.2.10.1: UN pressure receptacles per 49 CFR Parts 173, 178, 180
- A3.3.2.10.2: Foreign cylinders per 49 CFR Part 178 or with competent authority approval
- A3.3.2.10.3: Cylinders with DOT Special Permit or Exemption
- A3.3.2.10.4: ICC-prefix cylinders (equivalent to DOT cylinders)

### A3.3.2.13 - Mounted Cylinders Alternative (Expansion)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.13
**Text:** "Cylinders, other than those identified in A3.3.2.7, containing non-flammable gases (e.g., oxygen, air, nitrogen) and fire extinguishers may be shipped secured in holders of equipment and protected from possible accidental damage with safety pin/clip installed."
**Impact:** Allows cylinders to remain mounted in equipment holders

### A3.3.2.16.1.3 - Steel Jacket Requirement for Flammable Cryogenics (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.16.1.3
**Text:** "The jacket covering the insulation on a cylinder used to transport any flammable cryogenic liquid must be made of steel."
**Impact:** Material restriction - steel only

### A3.3.2.16.1.5 - Aluminum Prohibition for Flammable Cryogenics
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.16.1.5
**Text:** "Do not install an aluminum valve, pipe, or fitting on any cylinder used to transport any flammable cryogenic liquid."
**Impact:** Absolute prohibition on aluminum components

### A3.3.2.7 - Cylinders Requiring Outer Packaging
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.7
**Cylinders Requiring Strong Outer Packaging:**
- DOT 2P, 2Q, 3E, 3HT
- Spherical type 4BA, 4D, 4DA, 4DS
- DOT 39
**Test Requirement:** Package must protect from 1.2 m (4 foot) drop on concrete or steel
**Exception:** DOT 4BA spherical cylinders may be mounted on warehouse pallets

## Section 8: Aircraft Limitations/Prohibitions

### A3.3.9.3 - Magnetized Material FORBIDDEN (Absolute)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.9.3
**Text:** "Any package that has a magnetic field strength of more than 0.00525 gauss measured at 4.5 m (15 ft) from any surface of the package is forbidden on military aircraft."
**Waiver:** None available - absolute prohibition
**Impact:** Must measure magnetic field strength; exceeding limit = cannot ship by military air

### A3.3.7.4.4 - Type B Radioactive Package Restrictions
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.4.4
**Do not offer for military airlift:**
- A3.3.7.4.4.1: Type B(U) or B(M) package with accessible surface temp >50°C (122°F)
- A3.3.7.4.4.2: Continuously vented Type B(M) packages requiring external cooling or operational controls
- A3.3.7.4.4.3: Any liquid pyrophoric radioactive materials

### A3.3.7.4.5 - Exclusive Use Radioactive Restrictions
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.4.5
**Text:** "Do not transport exclusive use shipments of packages having a surface radiation level in excess of 2 mSv/h (200 mrem/h) except by special arrangement."

### A3.3.7.12.3 - Radioactive Subsidiary Hazard Restrictions
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.12.3
**Restrictions:**
- Division 4.2 (PG I) subsidiary: Must use Type B packages
- **Division 2.1 subsidiary: FORBIDDEN on passenger aircraft**
- **Division 2.3 subsidiary: FORBIDDEN on passenger AND cargo aircraft without waiver/CAA**

### A3.3.7.5.4 - Radioactive Separation from Persons (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.5.4
**Requirements:**
- Category II-Yellow and III-Yellow: Minimum 2 pallet positions (176 inches) from persons/animals (T-0)
- If total TI >50: Minimum 9 m (30 feet) separation (T-0)

### A3.3.7.5.5 - Maximum Radioactive Limits per Aircraft
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.5.5
**Limits:**
- Maximum TI per package: 10
- Maximum CSI per aircraft: 100
- Maximum TI per aircraft: 200

### A3.3.9.6.2-6.4 - Dry Ice Passenger Restrictions
**Paragraphs:** A3.3.9.6.2-6.4
**Requirements:**
- Seat passengers forward of dry ice, minimum 1 full pallet position separation
- Passengers/crewmembers cannot occupy same pallet position as dry ice
- No dry ice (exceeding carry-on quantities) in upper deck compartments

## Section 9: Exemptions from Requirements

### A3.1.1.1 - UN Specification Test Exemptions
**See Section 6 above** - 9 categories exempt from UN performance specification testing

### A3.2.1.1 - Flameless Ration Heaters (MRE Component)
**Paragraph:** A3.2.1.1
**Exemption:** FRH containing ≤8 grams magnesium-iron alloy, packed as MRE component, regardless of quantity shipped = NOT REGULATED
**Condition:** Must be packed WITH the MRE; shipped separately = regulated as hazmat

### A3.3.3.7 - Pads/Swabs Flammable Liquid Exemption
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.7
**Exemption:** Pads, swabs, rags soaked with flammable liquid and sealed in bag = NOT REGULATED
**Conditions:**
- No free liquid
- Each bag/packet contains ≤10 ml PG II or PG III flammable liquid
**Not Exempt:** PG I flammable liquid or >10 ml PG II/III → ship as UN3175

### A3.3.3.8 - Alcoholic Beverages Exemption
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.8
**Text:** "Alcoholic beverages in packagings of five liters or less are not subject to the requirements of this manual."
**Condition:** ≤5 liters per packaging

### A3.3.8.4 - Hypochlorite Solution Exemption
**Paragraph:** A3.3.8.4
**Exemption:** Not regulated if tested and does not meet corrosive material criteria
**Reference:** Test per 49 CFR Section 173.137
**Impact:** Liquid bleaches may be non-regulated if tested

### A3.3.9.2.3 - Excepted Lithium Batteries
**Paragraph:** A3.3.9.2.3
**Exemption Criteria:**
- Lithium ion cells ≤20 Wh, batteries ≤100 Wh
- Lithium metal cells ≤1g, batteries ≤2g
- Must meet packaging and marking requirements
**Not Subject To:** All other requirements of manual when properly prepared

### A3.3.9.5 - Unregulated Engines/Fuel Components
**Paragraph:** A3.3.9.5
**Items Exempt When Drained, Purged, No Other Hazmat:**
- A3.3.9.5.1: Vehicles/internal combustion engines (fuel systems completely drained, purged, sealed) (T-0)
- A3.3.9.5.2: Aircraft engines drained/purged per tech manual, no other hazmat
- A3.3.9.5.3: Fuel tanks/cells drained, purged, sealed per tech directive
- A3.3.9.5.4: Serviceable fuel assemblies (carburetors, pumps, filters) drained/purged

### A3.3.6.2.4 - Category B Infectious Substances (Non-Culture)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.6.2.4
**Exemption:** Category B infectious substances (other than cultures) assigned UN3373 = excepted from all other requirements
**Conditions:**
- Package marked "Biological Substance, Category B" (≥6mm)
- UN3373 in square-on-point marking on contrasting background
- Package meets A10.9 requirements

### A3.3.2.9 - Unregulated Compressed Gases
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.9
**Not Regulated:**
- A3.3.2.9.1: Inflated tires (≤rated inflation pressure)
- A3.3.2.9.2: Inflated sports balls
- A3.3.2.9.3: Aerosols, non-flammable gas, ≤50 ml capacity
- A3.3.2.9.4: Carbonated beverages
- A3.3.2.9.5: Refrigerating machines with ≤12 kg nonflammable LG, ≤12 L ammonia solution, or ≤100 g flammable LG
- A3.3.2.9.6: Containers pressurized with non-flammable gas ≤40 psia at 20°C per tech directive
- A3.3.2.9.7: Empty cylinders per A3.1.16.2
- A3.3.2.9.8: Accumulators meeting specific criteria
- A3.3.2.9.9: Passenger restraint systems per A6.3.6
- A3.3.2.9.10: Articles with ≤100 mg inert gas, ≤1 g per package

### A3.3.7.13 - Radioactive Excepted Packages
**Paragraph:** A3.3.7.13
**Exemption:** UN2908 (Empty), UN2909, UN2910, UN2911 = NOT REGULATED when prepared per A11.5 and marked per A14.4.6.2
**Note:** If meets criteria of other class/division, prepare per that ID number

### A3.3.1.10 - Grandfathered Explosives
**Paragraph:** A3.3.1.10
**Exemption:** Government-owned Class 1 packaged before 1 January 1990 = exempt from UN specification requirements
**Condition:** Must annotate Key 19 "Government-owned goods packaged before 1 January 1990"

### A3.4 - Household Goods Engine Equipment
**Paragraph:** A3.4
**Exemption:** Engine power-driven equipment (motorcycle, moped, lawnmower, boat, snowmobile) = NOT REGULATED when:
- Completely drained of fuel
- Run until engine stalls
- All oil and cooling fluids drained
- Fuel tanks/lines open ≥24 hours before pickup
- Non-spillable batteries disconnected, terminals taped, secured upright

## Section 10: Material Compatibility Requirements

### A3.1.3 - General Compatibility (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.3
**Requirements:**
- Containers must not react with or be decomposed by contents (T-0)
- Plastic containers/liners must prevent permeation (T-0)
- Plastic packaging for liquid hazmat must pass 49 CFR Part 173, Appendix B test (T-0)

### A3.3.2.2.1 - Cylinder Content Compatibility (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.3.2.2.1
**Text:** "Cylinders or spherical pressure vessels must not contain gases or materials capable of combining chemically so as to endanger their serviceability."
**Waiver Tier:** T-0

### A3.3.4.1 - Class 4 with Corrosives
**Paragraph:** A3.3.4.1
**Restriction:** "Do not pack Class 4 material in the same outer packaging with corrosive liquids, unless the corrosive liquids are in bottles cushioned by incombustible, non-reactive absorbent material. Place the cushioned bottles in tightly closed metal containers."
**Exception:** Material ≤118 ml (4 oz) in securely closed metal cans for complete fumigant

### A3.3.5.2 - Class 5 with Corrosives
**Paragraph:** A3.3.5.2
**Restriction:** "Do not pack Class 5 materials in the same outer packaging with corrosive liquids, unless the corrosive liquids are in bottles cushioned by incombustible absorbent material in tightly closed metal containers."
**Exception:** Material ≤118 ml (4 oz) in securely closed metal cans for complete fumigant

### A3.3.6.2.6 - Division 6.2 Combination Packaging Contents
**Paragraph:** A3.3.6.2.6
**Restriction:** Division 6.2 packaging may not contain other hazmat EXCEPT:
- Refrigerants (dry ice, liquid nitrogen) per 49 CFR 173.196
- Anticoagulants for blood/plasma stabilization
- Small quantities Class 3/8/9 or PG II/III (≤30 ml or 30g per inner, ≤4L or 4kg per outer) as preservatives - these are NOT REGULATED

### A3.3.6.1.6 - Toxic Materials with Food
**Paragraph:** A3.3.6.1.6
**Restriction:** "Do not place any liquid toxic material on the same 463L pallet with foodstuffs or rations."

## Section 11: Closure and Inner Packaging Requirements

### A3.1.6 - Closures (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.6
**Requirements:**
- Close packages as specified in test report, packaging instruction, drawing, or manufacturer's instructions (except A28.2.2) (T-0)
- Friction-type devices (stoppers, corks) must be held securely, tightly, effectively (T-0)
- **Screw-type closures on hazardous liquid containers (except UN jerricans) must be secured with:** (T-0)
  - Pressure-sensitive tape
  - Self-shrinking plastic
  - Wire
  - Integral locking cap
  - Other positive means to prevent loosening from vibration/temperature change

### A3.1.10 - Inner Packaging (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.10
**Requirements:**
- Pack, secure, cushion inner packagings to prevent breakage/leakage and control movement
- When partial contents removed, fill voids to ensure tight pack
- Cushioning must not react dangerously with inner packaging contents (T-0)
- Inner packagings required as specified by packaging paragraph
- See Attachment 20 for absorbent, closure, cushioning requirements

### A3.1.4 - Leak Containment (Liner) Requirements (T-0)
**Paragraph:** A3.1.4
**Requirements:**
- Leak containment required for hazardous liquids when outer packaging is not liquid-tight (T-0)
- Exception: Does not apply to overpacks for air shipment consolidation only
- Use leak-proof liner, plastic bag, or equivalent per A3.1.2
- **Items drained and purged that are susceptible to leaking purging fluid must also be contained in liner** (T-0)

## Section 12: Fuel Draining/Purging Requirements

### A3.3.3.4 - Fuel-in-Tank Limitations
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.4
**Requirements:**
- Limit fuel to minimum in vehicles, self-propelled units, wheeled engine-powered SE, all SE types
- Commanders consider fuel availability at destination and operational requirements
- Ship with less than maximum when possible
- During redeployments, limit fuel unless mission readiness affected
- Preparer ensures unnecessary fuel drained prior to shipment

### A3.3.3.5 - Bulk Fuel Draining/Purging by Flash Point
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.5
**Critical Rule:**

| Flash Point | Requirement |
|------------|-------------|
| **< 38°C (100°F)** | **MUST PURGE** (regardless of tech manual) |
| **≥ 38°C (100°F)** | DRAIN only (unless tech manual requires purge) |

**Additional Requirements:**
- A3.3.3.5.3: Provide air circulation in cargo compartment of pressurized aircraft
- A3.3.3.5.4: External aircraft fuel tanks - drain AND purge ALL fuel from tank, stand-pipe, internal lines

### A3.3.3.6 - Equipment Fuel Leakers List
**Paragraph:** A3.3.3.6
**Items That MUST Be Drained of Fuel:**
- MC-1A and MC-2A compressors (Exception: MC-1A model 2MC-1A per T.O. 34Y1-56-71 may have fuel-in-tank)
- MA-3 air conditioner
- H-1 heater
- USCSMK Boston Whaler boat (Exception: US Navy PBL may have fuel-in-tank)
- USMC River Assault Craft (RAC)
- **All commercial SE** (T-0)

### A3.3.9.4.1 - Vehicle/SE Fuel Level Determination
**Paragraph:** A3.3.9.4.1
**Priority:**
1. Technical directive (cannot exceed packaging paragraph limits)
2. Packaging paragraph if no tech directive
**Measurement Methods:**
- Fuel gauge
- Graduated dip stick (if no operational gauge)
- Drain/siphon tank (if no positive measurement available)
- May refill to appropriate level in inspector presence (A28.1.2)

### A3.3.9.5 - Unregulated When Drained/Purged
**Paragraph:** A3.3.9.5
**Requirements for Exemption:**
- A3.3.9.5.1: Fuel systems including carburetors, pumps, controls, fuel tanks must be completely drained, purged, sealed with pressure seal type plug/caps with gaskets and O-rings (T-0)
- A3.3.9.5.4: Use barrier bag for residual purging fluid; mark purging fluid type and flash point on outer container

## Section 14: Solids in Liquid Packaging Rules

### A3.1.12 - Capacity Multipliers for Different PG Combinations
**Paragraph:** A3.1.12
**Base Rule:** Single/composite packaging tested for liquids may be filled with solid to gross mass (kg) = rated capacity (L) x specific gravity (or 1.2 if not marked)

**Multiplier Table:**

| Packaging Test Level | Contents | Multiplier |
|---------------------|----------|------------|
| PG I Liquid | PG II Solid | 1.5 x (capacity x SG) |
| PG I Liquid | PG III Solid | 2.25 x (capacity x SG) |
| PG II Liquid | PG III Solid | 1.5 x (capacity x SG) |

**Example:** A PG I liquid container rated at 20 L with SG of 1.2 could hold:
- PG II solid: 20 x 1.2 x 1.5 = 36 kg
- PG III solid: 20 x 1.2 x 2.25 = 54 kg

## Inspector Checklist - Critical Override Rules

### Pre-Inspection Questions

- [ ] Does material have subsidiary hazard of 4.1, 4.2, 4.3, 5.1, or 8? If yes + PG III → verify PG II packaging (A3.1.9)
- [ ] Is this Class 4, 5, or 8 liquid PG III? → verify PG I or II packaging
- [ ] Does package contain magnetized material? → measure at 4.5m, must be ≤0.00525 gauss
- [ ] Is this a cryogenic liquid? → verify steel jacket (if flammable), no aluminum components (if flammable), venting instructions
- [ ] Is material radioactive with subsidiary hazard? → check aircraft restrictions

### Packaging Verification

- [ ] UN specification packaging required? → check if material falls under A3.1.1.1 exemptions
- [ ] Combination packaging? → verify inner packaging meets 95 kPa (or 75 kPa for PG III Class 3/6.1)
- [ ] Single packaging with liquid? → verify meets hydraulic pressure test requirements
- [ ] Screw-type closure on liquid container? → verify secured with tape/plastic/wire/locking cap
- [ ] Class 1 material? → verify PG I or II packaging
- [ ] DOT 5L jerrican with fuel? → REJECT (prohibited for air shipment)

### Fuel/Engine Verification

- [ ] Vehicle with bulk fuel tank, flash point <38°C? → must be PURGED (not just drained)
- [ ] Equipment on fuel leakers list (MC-1A/2A, MA-3, H-1, Boston Whaler, RAC, commercial SE)? → must be drained
- [ ] Claiming drained/purged exemption? → verify fuel systems sealed with pressure seal plugs/caps with gaskets and O-rings

### Dry Ice Verification

- [ ] Know aircraft type and configuration? → verify against aircraft-specific limits
- [ ] Passengers aboard? → apply passenger limits and separation requirements
- [ ] AMC contract aircraft? → max 440 lbs without carrier approval

### Radioactive Material Verification

- [ ] Category II-Yellow or III-Yellow? → verify separation from persons (2 pallet positions minimum)
- [ ] Total TI on aircraft >50? → verify 9m separation from persons
- [ ] Subsidiary hazard Division 2.1? → forbidden on passenger aircraft
- [ ] Subsidiary hazard Division 2.3? → forbidden on all aircraft without waiver
- [ ] Type B(U) or B(M)? → verify surface temp ≤50°C, no continuous venting

### Lithium Battery Verification

- [ ] Claiming excepted status? → verify cell/battery size limits (Table A3.5)
- [ ] Package weight? → max 30 kg unless packed with/in equipment
- [ ] Lithium battery mark applied? → required unless button cells in equipment or ≤4 cells/2 batteries in equipment

### Exemption Verification

- [ ] Claiming FRH exemption? → verify ≤8g Mg-Fe alloy AND packed with MRE
- [ ] Claiming alcoholic beverage exemption? → verify ≤5L per packaging
- [ ] Claiming pad/swab exemption? → verify ≤10ml, PG II or III, no free liquid, sealed bag
- [ ] Claiming engine/fuel component exemption? → verify completely drained, purged, sealed, no other hazmat
